A **physical device** is a real iPhone, iPad or Android phone plugged into a worker. Once an operator **enrols** it, agents lease it with `--physical`, the same way they lease a simulator: the lease has a TTL, is renewed and is released.

## Enrolment

Enrolling a device records its model, OS version, whether it is a phone or a tablet, and the list of apps on it:

- On iOS, the apps installed by Xcode or `devicectl`. App Store and TestFlight apps are not on the list, and Simlock never touches them.
- On Android, the user-installed apps.

Simlock knows the device by its UDID (iPhone, iPad) or adb serial (Android). An iPad is a `tablet`; every Android device is a `phone`. On Android the OS version is the API level.

Simlock never leases a device that is not enrolled. A worker can enrol phones on its own when its operator turns on `devices.autoEnroll`. An Android phone that adb reaches over the network can be enrolled when the worker turns on `android.networkDevices`; its address is then its ID.

## What happens when a lease ends

```text
enrol: apps A, B recorded
lease: agent installs C, D ─► lease ends ─► C, D uninstalled ─► ready
```

When a physical lease is released or expires, Simlock uninstalls every app it can see that was not on the device at enrolment. It does **not**:

- touch App Store or TestFlight apps on iOS;
- reset settings, files, accounts or keychain items;
- clear the data of an app that was there at enrolment;
- reinstall an app from enrolment that has gone missing. It is reported, not put back.

To keep an app installed on purpose, the operator enrols the device again after installing it, so the app joins the list.

<Aside type="danger">
Every app that was not on the device at enrolment is uninstalled after each lease. Do not enrol, or lease, a phone that holds an app you want to keep, unless that app was on it at enrolment.
</Aside>

## What Simlock never does

- **It never erases or resets the device.**
- **It never deletes a physical device.** Cleanup, `simlock nuke` and `nuke --delete-devices` leave it enrolled. Removing a device only makes Simlock forget it; nothing is sent to the device.
- **It does not hide the device from other tools.** Simulators are kept apart from the rest of the machine; phones are not. Every tool on the worker sees an enrolled device, so an agent can reach a phone it did not lease. Tell agents to use only the device their lease names.

<Aside type="caution">
Enrol a device in one Simlock installation only. A second installation would uninstall the first one's apps whenever it ends a lease.
</Aside>

## Leasing a phone

- **`--device` matches the model name**, in any letter case. **`--class`** is `phone` or `tablet`; with neither you get a `phone`.
- **`--os` matches exactly.** `--os 18` does not match 18.4.1; write a range such as `">=18 <19"` for any 18.x.
- **Slim mode, image tags and downloads do not apply** to a phone.
- **A request no enrolled device could ever match fails at once** with `UNKNOWN_MODEL`. One that matches a leased, cleaning or absent device waits for it, in a line of its own.
- **A lease without `--physical` never gets a phone.** A worker can serve only phones on a platform (`<platform>.virtualDevices` set to `false`); it then refuses every lease without `--physical` on that platform with `UNKNOWN_MODEL`.
- **A physical lease survives a daemon restart.**

`simlock simctl` and `simlock adb` reach simulators and emulators only. For a phone, you use the platform's own tools with the values from the grant.

## Device states

| State | Means | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ready` | Enrolled, connected and free. | It can be leased. |
| `leased` | An agent holds it. | Nobody else gets it. |
| `absent` | Not reachable for about a minute: `not-present` (unplugged, or a different phone at a network address), `untrusted`, or `locked`. | A lease on it ends as `device-lost`. When it is back, Simlock cleans it and grants it to the next waiting request. |
| `quarantined` | The leftover apps could not be uninstalled after a lease. | Nobody can lease it. Simlock retries in the background; if it keeps failing, it unenrols the device and deletes nothing else on it. |

- **A locked iPhone or iPad counts as absent.** It is not enrolled or leased while it asks for its passcode.
- **Simlock cannot tell when an Android phone is locked**, so a locked Android phone is still leased.
- `simlock status` shows an absent or quarantined device with its reason.
